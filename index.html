Beyond Binary Pass/Fail Testing

Standard preclinical models often identify that a neural implant failed, but miss why.

ACCREVIA bridges this diagnostic gap. We combine multi-scale electrophysiology, sub-micron histopathology, and computational modeling to elucidate micro-level tissue-device interactions, ensuring your bioelectronic device performs reliably across chronic lifetimes.
